📜 ATHANDWE LOANS – Terms & Conditions

1. Application
Customers must provide accurate and complete information when applying for a loan.

2. Verification
ATHANDWE LOANS may verify customer identity, income, banking information and supporting documents before making a lending decision.

3. Documents
A 3-month bank statement is required for each loan application. Payslips may also be required depending on employment status and the application.

4. Approval
Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Applications are subject to assessment and verification.

5. Fees and Interest
Applicable interest, initiation fees and service fees will be disclosed to the customer before the loan agreement is accepted.

6. False Information
Applications containing false, misleading or fraudulent information may be rejected.

7. Repayment
Customers must repay the approved loan according to the agreed repayment schedule.

8. Privacy
Customer information must be handled securely and used for legitimate business and loan-processing purposes.

9. Final Agreement
The signed loan agreement will contain the final applicable terms, amounts, fees, interest and repayment obligations.

These are general website terms and should be reviewed and adapted for your business and applicable South African laws before the system is used for real lending.
